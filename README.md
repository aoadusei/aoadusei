### 🔬 Research Focus & Areas of Interest
My research sits at the intersection of **Applied Mathematics, Computer Vision, and Biomedical Signal Processing**:
* **Non-Contact Physiological Sensing:** Developing spatiotemporal deep learning and appearance-invariant computer vision frameworks for video-based pediatric respiratory rate estimation.
* **Longitudinal Clinical Phenotyping:** Unsupervised clustering and dimensionality reduction on pediatric growth and craniofacial datasets.
* **Topological Data Analysis (TDA):** Applying persistent homology and manifold geometries to high-dimensional biological data.
